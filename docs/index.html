03

Evidence

Three results carry the paper.

01

Measurement geometry matters.

Complementary excitation reduces posterior spread by a factor of 10.4 relative to repeated low-level measurements.

02

Physical transport works.

The median coordinate defect falls from the 10⁻² to the 10⁻⁸ range, while the map remains orientation preserving over all 96 tested samples.

03

Locality matters.

High-order transport is regular over the tested auxiliary region, but degrades near a competing-basin boundary.

04

Potential applications

Where this structure could become useful.

Motivated applications, not validated claims of the current paper.

Nonlinear system identification

Acoustic, structural and vibroacoustic models with interpretable parameters.

Calibration with uncertainty

Problems with correlated or strongly anisotropic parameter posteriors.

Expensive inverse models

When a useful local surrogate is cheap but direct nonlinear posterior learning is not.

Experiment design

Select excitations and observations that expose identifiable directions before inference.

Local multibasin inference

Multiple local transports where a single globally regular map is unrealistic.

Hybrid digital twins

Surrogate uncertainty combined with explicit nonlinear physical correction.
